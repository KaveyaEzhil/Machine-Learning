## Climate-Resilient Crop Yield Forecasting & Risk Mitigation

**Target Audience**: Agricultural Ministry Officials, District Extension Officers, Policy Analysts  
**Course & Project**: ITA0613 Machine Learning Pipeline  
**Author**: Kaveya E (`IET Assignment kaveya E`)

### Executive Summary
Escalating climate variability threatens regional crop yields and food security. Using historical agro-climatic data across 12,500 records from multiple agricultural zones, our machine learning pipeline provides early yield forecasting without relying on black-box commercial software.

---

### Key Data-Driven Insights

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| CLIMATE FACTOR       | OBSERVED YIELD IMPACT       | CRITICAL POLICY ACTION       |
+----------------------+-----------------------------+------------------------------+
| Max Temp > 37°C      | -450 kg/ha per °C increase  | Promote heat-tolerant seed   |
| Rainfall Anomaly < -1| -38% fertilizer efficiency  | Initiate micro-irrigation    |
| Organic Carbon > 0.75| +520 kg/ha drought buffer   | Subsidize compost & biochar  |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### Policy Recommendations
1. **Early Warning Risk Triggers**: Mandate automated crop relief releases when district Rainfall Anomaly Index drops below -1.0.
2. **Resilient Seed Varieties**: Shift district sowing recommendations toward heat-resistant wheat and rice cultivars in high-GDD zones.
3. **Soil Health Campaigns**: Incentivize soil organic carbon enhancement to increase soil moisture retention during dry spells.
